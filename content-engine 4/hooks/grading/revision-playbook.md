# The Revision Playbook

The rubric names the failure; this file prescribes the fix. Two levels: hook-level (any layer, any format) and script-level (the full body of a video, carousel, or post). Every fix ends with a full regrade against the rubric, not just the failed criterion, because fixes can break neighbors.

## Part 1: Hook-level fixes

**Throat-clearing** ("Hey guys, welcome back," any wind-up)
Fix: delete sentence one entirely. Regrade starting from what was sentence two; it's usually the real hook. If nothing survives the deletion, there was no hook.

**Could-be-anyone** (generic, no audience gate)
Fix: the specificity ladder, one rung at a time, regrading after each: add the audience > add the place > add the number > add the constraint. "Business owners waste ad money" > "Lansing HVAC owners waste ad money" > "waste 40% of ad spend" > "on clicks outside their service area." Stop climbing when it grades A.

**Unbackable claim** (hype without proof)
Fix: walk the trust ladder DOWN to the highest receipt actually held, then resize the claim to fit the receipt. Own a client result? Claim what the result proves. Own only an observation? Frame it as a pattern seen, not a law. Never fix this by softening language while keeping the claim; fix the claim.

**No curiosity gap and no recognition** (nothing pulls)
Fix: the withholding move: name the outcome, hide the mechanism ("added three text files, hit page one" works BECAUSE the files aren't named). If the content is relatable rather than revealing, convert to a recognition hook instead (Mirror family); don't force a gap where the audience wants to feel seen.

**Needs-context** (confusing without the video/image)
Fix: front-load the noun in the first five words. Exception: deictic hooks ("this and this") are allowed ONLY with a planned visual pairing; if the visual isn't locked, rewrite verbal.

**Buried payoff** (the good part arrives late in the line)
Fix: invert the sentence; the payoff phrase moves into the first five words, setup trails. Then check the first three words as their own mini-hook again.

**Audience mismatch** (right hook, wrong room)
Fix: either regate (add an Identity Gate opener from Connection) or admit the goal was mischosen and repull from the correct goal section. Don't sand the hook to fit; refit the hook.

**Platform truncation fail** (dies at the fold)
Fix: compress to the platform number (140 LinkedIn, 125 IG, 80 Facebook), power word pushed forward. A cliffhanger cut mid-tension passes; cut mid-setup fails.

**Layer misalignment** (text hook and spoken hook never connect)
Fix: the bridge sentence: the first spoken line must contain the text hook's key noun or claim. Documented failure: the 0.9x awareness video, strong text, strong analogy, no bridge. One sentence fixes it.

**Weak first three words**
Fix: recut so words one to three carry either the command, the number, the name, or the gate. "So I wanted to talk about pricing" > "Pricing is lying to you."

## Part 2: Script-level fixes (the body)

**The flat stretch** (more than 8 seconds, or 3+ slides/lines, without a turn)
Fix: insert a secondary hook from the structure.md technique table, DIFFERENT type than the last one used: a number, a prop, a partial truth flip, a mid-CTA, an anti-example.

**Same-type re-hooks back to back** (two stats, two questions in a row)
Fix: swap the second for a different family; repetition of type reads as a list, and lists leak viewers.

**The broken compression promise** (a Time Collapse hook followed by rambling)
Fix: cut the body to standalone one-line directives, no connective tissue, per the verified 13-years format. If a section can't compress, it's its own future post.

**The tension leak** (payoff lands too early, back half dies)
Fix: move the reveal one delay beat later, or open a second loop before closing the first ("and that's not even the expensive mistake").

**Missing or misplaced ramp**
Fix: run conversion/funnel.md; verified placements are mid-content right before the final step (the sunk-cost slot) or the second-to-last slide. A ramp on a carousel's final slide moves up one.

**The flat ending**
Fix: the loop close: reprise the opening element bigger (the 511x stat reprise, the ring-structure couple, the receipt slide's cover callback). Endings that just stop leave the loop open and the save unearned.

**Runtime mismatch**
Fix: cut to the goal's verified band in formats/video.md. Virality content over 60 seconds and lead content over 60 seconds are both usually one video hiding two.

## Part 3: Revision discipline

1. Two revision passes maximum per piece. If it isn't at A or B after two, the concept is the problem: pull a new batch from the goal section instead of polishing a C.
2. Every fix triggers a FULL regrade; local fixes create global failures.
3. Repeated failure types get logged in the rubric's validation log; three occurrences of the same failure across pieces means the generation step needs the fix, not the revision step. That log feeds the quarterly review.
4. Post-publication failures follow the re-post rule: a flopped piece with an A-grade hook was likely under-distributed; a flopped piece with a B usually earns its revision pass and a repost with the better cover or opener.
