# Verbal Hook Templates

Fill-in-the-blank spoken openers, organized by the five goals and the pattern names in patterns.md. Every template is written for the EAR: read it aloud before using it. Slots take any niche.

Templates marked (v) are verified madlibs extracted directly from analyzed winners; the rest are adapted from the archive library into spoken form.

---

## Goal 1: Capturing Leads

### The Command Open
- (v) If you have [any type of business/asset], [take this specific action]: [the action, restated in detail].
- (v) [Do this] to that [thing] and [do it] again. [Repeat verbatim.]
- (v) Never [common activity] again without [these # things].
- Stop [common action] right now, and do [replacement action] instead.

### The Money-Metric List
- (v) Here are [#] [niche] business ideas that will make you [wealth outcome], and how much each of them can make you per year.
- (v) [#] boring [things] that make a lot more than you think.
- [#] [services] people in [niche] happily pay [$ floor] for, and what each costs you to start.

### The Library Tease
- (v) These [resources] go viral time and time again. Use them in your next [project].
- (v) [Category label]. [Next category label]. [Next.] (title-card directory style)
- (v) Here are [#] [resources] you must [consume] to instantly [result].
- I've been collecting [resources] for [time]. Here are the only [#] worth your attention.

### The Stakes Gate
- (v) You will never [desirable outcome] if you don't learn how to [skill].
- If you can't [basic test], you're not ready for [desired thing], and here's how to fix that today.

## Goal 2: Authority

### The Proxy Open
- (v) This [person] wrote on [platform]: "[surprising or bold claim]."
- (v) I [achieved milestone] and then [personal tragedy/reversal]. (high-stakes personal variant)
- (v) I'm a [title]. My [thing] went from [before metric] to [after metric]. (guest-speaks-first variant)
- My client [specific result] without [expected pain]. Here's the exact play.

### The Compression Promise
- (v) I'm gonna compress [long period] of [niche] advice into this one video.
- (v) It took me [years] to learn this, but I'll teach it to you in less than [tiny time].
- [#] lessons from [#] [units of experience], in [short time]. No fluff.

### The Insider Percentile
- (v) If you [consume this thing], you will know more than [99]% of [group].
- After [# of audits/projects], I can tell you the one thing the top [1]% of [group] do differently.

### The Parallel Fact Stack
- (v) [Entity] owns no [asset]. [Entity] owns no [asset]. [Entity] owns no [asset]. [Thesis.]
- [Famous case] didn't have [expected advantage]. Neither did [case] or [case]. Here's what they had instead.

## Goal 3: Virality

### The Micro Binary
- (v) [Option A] or [option B]?
- Would you rather [respectable hard thing] or [embarrassing easy thing]? Be honest.

### The Spoken News-Jack
- (v) [Authority] just officially killed [industry standard].
- (v) Most [audience] will notice [symptom] before they understand why.
- (v) I built my entire [career/business] on a rule [authority] just deleted.

### The Fragment Stack
- (v) [Strenuous task]. [Duration]. [Constraint].
- [Goal]. [Budget]. [Deadline]. Let's see if it's possible.

### The Rule-Setting Game
- (v) Okay, here's what we're gonna do. We're gonna [action], but [constraint].
- The rules are simple: [rule one], [rule two], and I can't [obvious shortcut].

## Goal 4: Connection

### The Identity Gate
- (v) If you're actual [specific high-stakes identity], like [definitive criterion of that identity]...
- This is only for the [audience] who [costly behavior nobody sees]. Everyone else can scroll.

### The Expectation Deflator
- (v) I hate when people [common question/assumption] like I'm out here [exaggerated expectation] or something.
- Everyone thinks [role] means [glamorous image]. Here's my actual [time period].

### The Mundane Cold Open
- (v) "Hey, can you [tiny request]? It's [absurdly convenient location]."
- "[Completely ordinary line of dialogue]" (then the scene escalates)

### The Role Peek Intro
- (v) Welcome to a day in the life of a [age]-year-old [identity] who [humble task] for a living.
- (v) All [they] wanted was [simple beginning], and now this is [their] life. (silent text variant)
- I'm a [age] [title], and I'm heading to [event]. Come with me.

### The Mission Ask
- (v) Can we [kind action] for a [someone] that's been [overlooked condition] and needs [small kindness]?
- Today's mission: find the [overlooked person/thing] in [place] and [act of service].

### The Soft Promise
- (v) I hope this story can change your life. When I was [age], I was [deictic grounding: right there/over there]...
- I don't tell this story often, but somebody watching needs it today.

## How to use this file

1. Goal first, then pattern (patterns.md), then pull 2 or 3 templates from that pattern here.
2. Fill slots with the most specific detail available, then READ IT ALOUD. If you stumble, the viewer scrolls.
3. Grade the first three words as their own mini-hook, then the full line against the rubric. Only A and B get filmed.
4. Write the caption separately from the written/ goal section; it is its own hook slot.
5. Script the rest of the video with structure.md: a re-hook every 5 to 8 seconds, different type each time.
