# Verbal Hook Examples

Real spoken hooks with real performance data, grouped by goal. Every entry: the spoken open, platform + numbers + link, pattern, why it worked, and an ADAPT IT line. No numbers, no entry.

---

## Goal 1: Capturing Leads

**"If you have any type of business, buy a domain name: your business name plus the word reviews."**
Instagram, 1.9M views, 91.3x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/92e87058-4e7c-420f-8c98-89aa263e828c
Pattern: Command Open. The instruction is the hook; the why comes after. Rooftop set matches the advice's status level.
ADAPT IT: "[If you have X], [do cheap specific action]: [the action restated]." Then explain, then the one catch.

**"Delete that video and post it again. Delete that video and post it again."**
TikTok, 7M views, 150.2x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/cd598dd0-b295-4f83-b359-d018b0c1c22c
Pattern: Command Open, repetition form. The verbatim repeat reads as urgent instruction; proof follows (500 to 500,000).
ADAPT IT: repeat any contrarian command twice, then logic, then your own before/after numbers.

**"Here are three business ideas for weddings that will make you a millionaire and how much each of them can make you per year."**
TikTok, 3.1M views, 177.1x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/a9fb568c-0548-4ccf-85a8-950a48e0099b
Pattern: Money-Metric List. Sister video ("3 boring businesses," 217.1x) reuses the identical skeleton: this is a repeatable series.
ADAPT IT: "[#] [niche] ideas that will [money outcome], and how much each makes per year." Pay one number per item.

**"These background songs go viral time and time again. Use them in your next video."**
Instagram, 764K views, 15,911 comments. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/ef7b2bd5-27da-43a1-b001-c4cd0ce97ef0
Pattern: Library Tease. Examples tiered by view level; the full 150+ library gated behind comment "Songs."
ADAPT IT: showcase 5 tiered samples of any resource, gate the full library behind a keyword.

## Goal 2: Authority

**"This guy wrote on LinkedIn, 'Okay guys, just informing everyone I'm the world's most renowned AI visibility expert.'"**
Instagram, 2.3M views, 94.2x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/4f324bd8-1d87-4274-b117-645b2a5feecf
Pattern: Proxy Open. Someone else's absurd claim carries the authority; the receipt (the actual post) is on screen in 2 seconds.
ADAPT IT: find a "glitch in the matrix" in your niche, tell it as a story, show the proof, then extract the reusable tactic.

**"I made $106 million and then my mum died."**
Instagram, 1.7M views, 4.9x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/324aa2cc-d72a-4b64-b72d-d34108df711e
Pattern: Proxy Open, high-stakes personal variant. Achievement and tragedy collide in one sentence; the caption runs a completely different hook.
ADAPT IT: "[major milestone], and then [reversal]." Only with true stakes; the tone of everything after must match.

**"I'm gonna compress 13 years of business advice into this one video."**
Instagram, 1.4M views, 4.0x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/91563388-a5d6-4ae6-a6c7-6d133c53ec46
Pattern: Compression Promise. Twelve standalone one-line directives follow; every sentence is screenshot-able.
ADAPT IT: "[years] of [niche] advice in [seconds]." Then deliver rules, not paragraphs.

**"Uber owns no cars. Airbnb owns no houses."** (on-screen text, no speech)
Instagram, 3.2M views, 649.4x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/9fbb5741-80cb-4570-a7b3-837b81694f3e
Pattern: Parallel Fact Stack, silent form. Six seconds total; the parallel grammar does the persuading.
ADAPT IT: "[Entity] has no [asset]" x3-5 in identical grammar, then one thesis line. Text card over relevant b-roll.

**"If you were to consume all the information I have on this piece of paper, you will know more than 99% of advertisers on Meta."**
TikTok, 114.8K views, 23.0x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/d5fec6df-fa27-4cb5-9f62-fdda7f8e512d
Pattern: Insider Percentile. The handwritten paper is the physical proof of depth; taps it while teaching.
ADAPT IT: "[consume this] and you'll know more than 99% of [group]," anchored to a visible artifact of your homework.

## Goal 3: Virality

Pattern: Spoken News-Jack. Highest niche outlier in the free dataset; sister hook "Most site owners will notice the drop before they understand why" hit 260x.
ADAPT IT: "[Authority] just killed [thing your audience relies on]" the day the news breaks. Receipt required.

**"Big house or little house?"**
YouTube Shorts, 2.1M views, 69.2x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/fa49bc94-e3d1-4d5f-94ae-327d1a03b0a9
Pattern: Micro Binary. Three words force an internal pick; a vivid scene follows instead of an answer; ring-structure ending revisits the opening couple.
ADAPT IT: "[A] or [B]?" then paint a specific persona mid-decision. Close by returning to them.

**"Challenge: the most painful tattoo spot—armpit. Two days in a row. No numbing cream."**

Pattern: Fragment Stack. Three fragments, three escalating constraints, action already running underneath.
ADAPT IT: "[Task]. [Duration]. [Constraint]." Start the attempt in frame one.

**"Okay, here's what we're gonna do. We're gonna... you're gonna cut, but we can't look."**

## Goal 4: Connection

**"If you're actual big boy self-employed, like 100% of your household income is derived from your business..."**
Instagram, 3.9M views, 2,153.5x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/a8dd35b0-5d33-4759-a63e-48d497150529
Pattern: Identity Gate. Eighteen seconds spent defining who's inside; then the unspoken burden; then "you already won by betting on yourself."
ADAPT IT: gate hard on your exact audience ("If you're actual [identity], like [criterion]"), name their invisible weight, reframe survival as victory.

**"I hate when people ask what I did on the weekend like I'm out here skydiving or something."**
Instagram, 18.1M views, 449.3x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/cac85e03-3cbc-4fa1-8505-5ba27a1fa03c
Pattern: Expectation Deflator. Five seconds of speech; the shredded-cheese specificity is what makes it travel.
ADAPT IT: "[I hate when people X] like I'm out here [absurd expectation]," then ONE embarrassingly specific true detail.

**"Hey can you pass me my sweater? It's right behind you."**
Instagram, 59.1M views, 62.0x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/63bab7e3-e43c-42d7-ae8f-9de92210021b
Pattern: Mundane Cold Open. The tiny request vs the five-ritual delay; the caption runs a sincere 23-years-married reflection in a different register.
ADAPT IT: open any skit with the most ordinary line of dialogue in your niche's daily life, then elaborate the resistance.

**"Welcome to the day in the life of a 32-year-old small business owner who makes coffee for a living."**
TikTok, 2.5M views, 49.0x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/ba4c3ef9-5c31-4d80-881e-9470cee821db
Pattern: Role Peek Intro. Age + identity + humble task; "my friend" repeated ~20 times becomes the signature.
ADAPT IT: "[age]-year-old [identity] who [humble concrete task] for a living," plus one repeatable phrase of your own.

**"Hello. Can we take out a dog that's been here for a while, maybe a little sad, needs a little pick-me-up?"**
Instagram, 29.5M views, 29.5x, 11% engagement (highest in pool). https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/568e86bd-25a1-426d-8146-a30d077d2561
Pattern: Mission Ask. Spoken to the shelter clerk, not the camera; backstory beat supplies the stakes; ends on a promise.
ADAPT IT: start a kind mission on camera by asking a third party for the overlooked one; let the viewer overhear.

**"I hope that this story can change your life."**
TikTok, 229.7K views, 44.1x, 14% engagement. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/49635616-1ea6-471a-adb6-bb8287ed8dbc
Pattern: Soft Promise. Deictic grounding ("walking right over there on that ridge") makes it physical; closes with explicit permission-giving.
ADAPT IT: soft promise in, real stakes in the middle, permission out ("this is me giving you permission to [action]").

## Goal 5: Educational

**"Just a reminder that 22% of the global population uses Gmail, and this is the easiest marketing hack that anybody can do."**
Instagram, 10.8M views, 511.1x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/9a9c6da5-1404-41ef-8461-883ac575cd58
Pattern: Stat Reminder. Opens on the stat, closes by reprising it bigger (75% US); exclusivity engine: easy + nobody does it.
ADAPT IT: "Just a reminder that [stat], and this is the easiest [hack] anybody can do." Reprise the stat at the end.

**"The reason you can't yap on camera is because you don't have a framework for yapping on camera."**
Instagram, 1.3M views, 65.9x, 13K comments. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/164041c4-c66a-47e8-be27-e1165d64d433
Pattern: Diagnosis. Acts out the failure as an anti-example, reveals the named framework, drops the lead magnet before the final step.
ADAPT IT: "The reason you can't [result] is you don't have a [framework] for it," then perform the failure, then name the cure.

**"If you're poor, try the buy nothing challenge."**
Instagram, 2M views, 5.5x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/89dca8ee-ebea-4a12-86d4-a87203d82778
Pattern: Named Challenge. Blunt call-out + program name + immediate concrete rules (wallet at home, pack lunch).
ADAPT IT: "If you're [state], try the [Name] Challenge," then three rules doable this week.

**"By far the worst thing that you can do right now is make value content. Let me explain."**
Instagram, 85.8K views, 4.3x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/27c865e6-a884-4834-ae2b-306e75306ba2
Pattern: Contrarian Flip + Bridge. "(2023)" date-stamp frames the habit as outdated; old-vs-new contrast lands immediately.
ADAPT IT: attack a praised practice, add "Let me explain," show old-vs-new, then redefine the term.

**"Here are 4 YouTube videos that you must watch to instantly make better content. And here's the catch, none of these have anything to do with social media."**
Instagram, 288K views, 14.5x, 4,924 comments. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/526b1128-f675-487d-8b67-24df455651ee
Pattern: Catch Twist + Library Tease. The twist at second 4 reframes the list and doubles as the first re-hook.
ADAPT IT: "[#] [resources] to [result]. And here's the catch: [twist true of every item]."

**"Never post on Instagram again without turning on these five settings."**
Instagram, 823K views, 370 comments + save CTA. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/14d3a697-78f1-4693-9772-1c0cb69438f7
Pattern: Command Open (stakes form) serving education. Mid-list proof bridge: "these next three settings got me millions of views for free."
ADAPT IT: "Never [activity] again without [# fixes]," and place your receipt bridge at the halfway droop.
