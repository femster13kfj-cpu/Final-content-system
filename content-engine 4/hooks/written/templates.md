# Written Hook Templates

Fill-in-the-blank openers, organized by the five content goals and the pattern names in patterns.md. Every template is niche-agnostic: swap the bracketed slots for any industry, audience, or offer. Curated and deduplicated from the PBL 1000 Viral Hooks library plus Sandcastles madlib extractions.

Slot key: [audience] [pain] [result] [time] [number] [$] [authority] [thing] [action] [title]

---

## Goal 1: Capturing leads

### The Direct Call-Out
- If you're a [audience] and you want [result] by [method], this is for you.
- If you're a [audience] who [pain] and you want [result], here's a simple [number]-step plan.
- [Audience]: if you're serious about [result], stop scrolling.
- If you're between [age/stage] and [age/stage] and you feel like [pain], read this.
- Want to be the first [result] in your family?
- [Audience], if you're serious about [next level], it's time to [accountability action].

### The Receipt Drop
- [$] in [time], and here's exactly how.
- This is what [$] gets you in [context].
- [Number] [things] in [time] without [expected cost or pain].
- I made [result] with under [$]. Here's the math.
- One [small thing] did [big measurable result]. I'll show you.

### The Stakes Warning
- If you don't [action] now, here's where you'll be by [time].
- You'll never get [result] in your [stage] if you skip these [number] things at [earlier stage].
- If you want to end up [pain], skip this post.
- The worst thing you can do for your [thing] is [common mistake]. Here's why.
- Whatever you do, do not let your [person/thing] [action].
- You have [high-stakes event] tomorrow and no time to [required action]. Here's how you save it.

### The Cost Reveal
- Here's the complete cost breakdown of [project].
- This is what [$] gets you in [location/context].
- For the price of [common purchase], you could have all of this.
- We saved [$] on [thing], and one thing we wish we'd done differently.
- How much does it really cost to [action]? Nobody talks about this.
- For the price of [common purchase] at [place], you could have [larger quantity] of [thing].

## Goal 2: Authority

### The Before/After
- I went from [before state] to [after state] in [time].
- My [thing] was [bad state]. [Small action] later, [good state].
- [Time] ago I was [pain]. Today [result].
- Take your [thing] from this to this.
- How long would it take you to go from [before] to [after]?

### The Credential Flex
- I'm a [title] who [specific proof]. Here's the one thing that separates the top [titles] from the rest.
- After [number] [units of experience], here are [number] things I'd never do.
- If I had [short time] to get [result] from scratch, this is exactly what I'd do.
- My client went from [before] to [after] without [expected pain]. Here's the exact play.
- I've [action] over [number] [things] in my life. These are the only [number] worth your time.

### The Experiment
- I tried [action] every day for [time]. Here's what happened.
- Is it possible to [result] in [time] with only [constraint]?
- I tested [claim] so you don't have to.
- We asked [number] [experts] the same question. [Number] responded.
- I bought every [product category] to find out which is actually worth your money.

## Goal 3: Virality

### The News-Jack
- [Authority] just killed [industry standard].
- [Authority] just announced [change], and it's going to [consequence] for [audience]. Here's what changed.
- I built my entire [career/business] on a rule [authority] just deleted.
- Most [audience] will notice [symptom] before they understand why. Here's what actually happened.
- If you're still doing [old method], you're playing by rules that no longer exist.

### The Myth Slayer
- Everyone tells you to [common advice], but nobody shows you how. Here's a [number]-second version you can save.
- They said "[famous advice]." That's a lie.
- You are not [negative self-label]. You were just never taught how to [skill].
- Stop [common action] if you actually want [result].
- [Thing] is not [common belief]. And I'm going to back that up with receipts.
- Let me de-influence you from [popular thing].
- Just because you've never [obvious failure], doesn't make you [claimed virtue]. Real [virtue] looks like this.
- Yes, [fear] is real. But not the kind you're thinking of.

### The Open Question
- What if I told you [surprising claim]?
- What happens when you ask [tool/person] to [extreme action]?
- Why does nobody talk about the cost of [common thing]?
- How long can you skip [action] before losing [result]?
- Would you rather [option A] or [option B]?

## Goal 4: Connection

### The Mirror
- POV: trying to [intended activity] when you [life context].
- You know that feeling when [hyper-specific shared moment].
- We all have that one [label] in our life.
- If you've ever secretly [action] and hoped no one noticed, this is for you.
- [Time] and [number] [life markers] later, here we are.

### The Story Drop
- [Time] ago I [surprising decision]. Here's how it's going.
- I don't have a backup plan, so this kind of needs to work.
- I just left my [$] [job/situation] to [risky move].
- [Time] ago my [person] told me "[quote]." It changed everything.
- Day [number] of [pursuit] until [dream result].

### The Role Peek
- Day in the life of a [identity with a twist].
- Come to work with me as a [title].
- This is what an average day looks like for a [title], [time] out from [event].
- I'm a [age/stage] [title], and I'm heading to [event/location].
- Do you ever [unusual situation]? Yeah, that's my job.

### The Series Loop
- Day [N] of [pursuit] until [dream result].
- Welcome back to [series name], where I [premise].
- Day [N] of trying to [goal] by [deadline], by [method].
- We are now at day [N] since starting [project]. Here's where things stand.
- Day 1 of starting over at [age/stage].

## Goal 5: Educational

### The Gatekeeper Open
- I think I just found the biggest [niche] cheat code.
- I've been gatekeeping this list of [things]. Today I'm giving it away.
- [Number] [resources] that will teach you more than any [expensive alternative].
- Here's the [thing] nobody in [industry] wants you to know.
- I worked at [company/industry] for [time] and now I'm sharing what they keep from customers.
- I'm a professional [industry] hacker, and here's every hack at [place].

### The Time Collapse
- It took me [years] to learn this, but I'll teach it to you in [seconds/minutes].
- In 60 seconds I'm going to teach you more about [thing] than you've learned in your entire life.
- [Number] lessons from [number] [experiences] in [time].
- 30 seconds of [industry] advice I'd give my best friend starting from scratch.
- After a decade of [action], here's what I wish someone had told me on day one.
- If I had to relearn [skill] from scratch, here are the only [number] things I'd keep.

### The Tier Split
- Bad [thing] vs good [thing] vs great [thing].
- Cheap vs expensive [thing]: can you tell the difference?
- This [option A] has [metric], and this [option B] has [metric]. Same price.
- [Industry] tier list for [year].
- Both of these [things] are identical except one thing. One got [result], the other got [worse result].
- One of theirs vs [number] of mine, at the exact same [metric].
- Same [product], two prices: [$A] vs [$B]. Here's what the difference actually buys.
- [Last year]: [old way]. [This year]: [new way].

## How to use this file

1. Name the content goal first, then pick the pattern from that goal's section in patterns.md.
2. Pull 2 or 3 templates from that pattern's section here.
3. Fill the slots with the most specific detail available. Specificity is the strongest single predictor of performance.
4. Check the platform truncation rules in the rubric (LinkedIn 140 characters safe, Instagram 125, Facebook aim for 80).
5. Grade every filled hook against the rubric. Only A and B survive.
