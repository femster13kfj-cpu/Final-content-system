# Written Hook Examples

Real hooks with real performance data. Every entry lists platform, performance, the pattern it maps to, and why it worked. This file is the proof layer: when generating hooks, these are the standard to beat.

Entry format:
- Hook (paraphrased where needed)
- Platform, views, outlier score, source link
- Pattern + why it worked
- ADAPT IT: one line on how to reskin the hook onto any topic or niche

---

## Verified winners (Sandcastles, July 2026)

**"ChatGPT is recommending my vehicle wrap shop to people searching for wraps in Utah. I didn't pay for it. I set it up with one file."**
Instagram, 1.7M views, 154.7x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/4e3153f2-46d1-4743-b729-d77ec825997d
Pattern: Receipt Drop + Gatekeeper Open. A specific business, a specific place, a shocking mechanism ("one file"), and an implied receipt. Three sentences, three escalations.
ADAPT IT: keep the triad. "[AI tool] is recommending my [business type] to people searching in [city]. I didn't pay for it. I set it up with one [small asset]."

**"My business was invisible on Google. I added three text files and now I'm on page one."**
Instagram, 354K views, 4.9x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/27978e12-0b52-4a1c-b356-cde2f571dffd
Pattern: Before/After. Both states are concrete, the bridge is absurdly small ("three text files"), which creates the curiosity gap.
ADAPT IT: any transformation with a tiny bridge. "My [thing] was [concrete bad state]. I [absurdly small fix] and now [concrete good state]."

**Google-just-killed-it opener (news-jack on an industry standard)**
TikTok, 472K views, 400.6x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/f4d4e0b0-3f6e-45a2-84bd-b07eae3e2e78
Pattern: News-Jack. Highest outlier score in the niche dataset. Urgency + authority + a threatened asset the audience depends on.
ADAPT IT: pair any real announcement with the asset it threatens. "[Authority] just killed [thing your audience relies on]."

**"Most site owners are going to notice the drop before they understand why."**
TikTok, 360K views, 260x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/6da3b8fc-5718-4a67-bfa5-dad162dbe5c5
Pattern: News-Jack + Stakes Warning. Names the audience, names the symptom they will personally feel, withholds the cause.
ADAPT IT: lead with the symptom, withhold the cause. "Most [audience] will notice [felt symptom] before they understand why."

**"What happens when you ask ChatGPT to wipe your entire digital identity off the internet?"**
Instagram, 3.4M views, 32.8x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/d25fdf50-59b1-48a7-88e8-60db8c5b3d45
Pattern: Open Question. Extreme action + universal stake (your own identity). The question cannot be left unanswered.
ADAPT IT: extreme action on a universal stake. "What happens when you ask [tool/person] to [extreme action on something deeply personal]?"

**"I built my entire career on a rule Google just deleted."**
TikTok, 100K views, 81.6x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/1962b1aa-4da6-4a86-8e41-e1726a100b57
Pattern: News-Jack + Story Drop. Personal stakes fused to industry news. The speaker's loss makes the change feel real.
ADAPT IT: fuse your own stakes to any rule change. "I built my entire [asset/career] on a rule [authority] just deleted."

**"Bad vs. Good vs. Great Scripts."**
Instagram, 118K views, 1.6x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/fb9b47dd-88ee-4ea2-bb09-27c04185d75c
Pattern: Tier Split. Five words. Works because the reader must see the tiers to know which one they are.
ADAPT IT: any craft with visible quality tiers. "Bad vs. good vs. great [deliverable]."

## Global reference points (outside the niche, for pattern universality)

**"13 years and 4 kids later, here we are"**
Instagram, 150M views, 4,220x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/7bd34744-f780-4485-84e9-9f7e0f9270e3
Pattern: Mirror. Pure recognition, near-zero curiosity loop. Proof that criterion 4's recognition path is real.
ADAPT IT: contrast a simple beginning with the chaotic now for any long shared journey. "[Time] and [milestones] later, here we are."

**"POV: trying to relax when you have kids"** (paraphrased)
Instagram, 33M views, 11.3x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/7d6c5be7-ba2c-4a35-b7b2-525f7b88ed8e
Pattern: Mirror. The situation is precise enough that the target person self-selects instantly.
ADAPT IT: any precise situation your audience lives daily. "POV: trying to [intent] when you [exact life context]."

**"The Most Painful Tattoo Spot on the Body with no Numbing cream"**
Instagram, 78M views, 50.6x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/cec8b154-2660-45bd-b03a-4bfad0cd9947
Pattern: Experiment with stakes. The constraint ("no numbing cream") is the hook inside the hook.
ADAPT IT: add a self-imposed constraint to any challenge. "[Hardest version of the task], with no [expected relief or shortcut]."

**"Most [superlative] [thing] in [location]" (hidden cricket ground reveal, DJI)**
Instagram, 55M views, 61.7x outlier. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/54151425-7502-41cf-8886-c22492b945d0
Pattern: Gatekeeper Open, superlative form. A brand account using a pure discovery hook. Proof the pattern works for companies, not just personal brands, and in any niche.
ADAPT IT: "Most [superlative] [thing] in [place]" fits any discovery your audience has never seen, from any account type.
