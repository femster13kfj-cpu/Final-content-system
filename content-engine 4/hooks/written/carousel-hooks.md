# Carousel Cover Hooks

The dedicated written-hook library for carousel covers. Separated because covers carry constraints no other slot has: the keyword-scaling rule (one power word carries the frame at 3x), the claim-not-topic rule, and mandatory pairing with the cover image via the Concept Engine.

Organized by Karoni's five cover mechanisms. Every entry lists its POWER WORD (what gets scaled in the type system) and a GOAL tag so the stacking rule holds: the caption, slides, and CTA of that carousel pull from the tagged goal's sections.

**The Pro Move (applies to every formula):** a hook is a CLAIM, not a topic. "Typography tips" describes; "your fonts are why it looks cheap" provokes. Add a tension word where the formula allows: costing, nobody, silently, before you post again, killing. Then run the 3-Second Test at the bottom of this file. 5/5 or don't post.

Niche note: blanks make every formula plug-and-play. Where a formula names an audience or platform outcome ("gets designers hired," "grow your account"), swap the slot for the client's audience and outcome; the skeleton is the asset.

## The 3-Second Test (the cover gate)

Score every cover before it ships. 5/5 or don't post; reposting with a better cover is always allowed (verified: the 150.2x re-post rule).
1. Can a stranger read it involuntarily (big enough)?
2. Is there ONE focal point?
3. Is it a claim, not decoration?
4. Under 10 words?
5. Would YOU stop scrolling?

## File rules
1. No power word, no cover.
2. Every entry carries a goal tag; the rest of the carousel pulls from that goal.
3. Setup fragments under 5 words each so the keyword dominates.
4. The Concept Engine picks the image AFTER the hook, pairing by outcome/tension.
5. Winners get promoted to written/examples.md with numbers, tagged carousel.
