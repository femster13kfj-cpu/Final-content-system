# Format: Carousel

Carousels use TWO hook layers: written (cover text and caption) and visual (cover image and slide design). No verbal layer. Both pull from the same goal section per the stacking rule.

## The three hook slots

1. **Cover text** (written/patterns.md, same goal): six words or fewer, the strongest written hook compressed. Numbers beat adjectives.
2. **Cover image** (visual/concept-engine.md): a creative literalization of the topic's best or worst outcome. This is the slot the Concept Engine exists for; run its full sequence every time.
3. **Caption** (written/patterns.md): its own separate hook, same rules as video captions: the hook inside Instagram's ~125-character window, comment-keyword line if the ramp is a keyword.

## Assembly sequence

1. Goal and topic.
2. Written hook chosen and compressed into cover text (Step 5 of the Concept Engine governs how text and image split the job: outcome vs mechanism, tension vs resolution, never redundant).
3. Cover image via the Concept Engine: two poles, pick the pole, six literalization moves, one-glance test, three concepts, grade, pick.
4. Slides built by the Slide Job Framework below: every slide has ONE job, and Instagram scores each job with a different metric. Slide layouts, typography, and climate rules live in visual/styles/slide-system.md.
5. Caption written last, separately.
6. Grade: the cover passes the 3-Second Test (written/carousel-hooks.md, 5/5 or don't post), and cover text, cover image, slide 2, and caption each pass the rubric, or the carousel goes back to assembly.

## The Slide Job Framework

| Slide | Job | Metric it serves |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (cover) | Open a loop: create the itch, never complete the thought | Swipe rate |
| 2 | The densest slide: first payoff fast, treated as a second cover | Dwell time |
| 3 to 6 | Delay + deliver: one idea per slide, END EACH SLIDE MID-THOUGHT | Completion |
| Second-to-last | The CTA: comment keyword / follow. People exit on the LAST slide, not here | Comments |
| Last | The receipt: everything compressed into one savable, shareable frame | Saves & shares |

Notes: the mid-thought slide endings are the carousel version of verbal/structure.md's re-hook rule. The receipt slide can carry the visual callback to the cover's image, closing the loop. The CTA placement (second-to-last) supersedes any earlier final-slide ramp guidance; the ramp check still applies, it just lives one slide earlier.

## Carousel-only mechanics

- **Slide one carries most of the swipe decision.** Budget effort accordingly: the cover gets the Concept Engine treatment; body slides get consistency.
- **4:5 sizing** (1080x1350); design for the grid crop too, since the cover doubles as the grid tile.
- **Slide count:** 6 to 10. Under 6 feels thin for a save; over 10 bleeds completion rate. The save is the carousel's core metric.
- **Text density:** cover 6 words or fewer; body slides 25 words or fewer; if a slide needs more, it's two slides.
- **The receipt slide:** the last slide is the savable compression of the whole carousel, and can carry the visual callback to the cover's image (the shop from the cover now AT the summit). The CTA lives one slide earlier. Exception: when the ramp is follow/save with no comment keyword, the CTA may close the deck (set 5 variant, slide-system.md Part 6).
- **The save trigger names the moment:** never a bare "save for later." Tie the save to the situation where they'll need it: "save this before your next client call," "save this before you run your next ad." Set in Shadows Into Light.
- **Comparisons inside one climate** use the red/green pill code (red pill = problem, green #1D9E75 pill = fix) per slide-system.md Part 6; the navy/light panel code remains the cross-climate default.
- **House style (locked per brand):** palette and type come from the active profile in `/clients/` and never change per post. Kyndle's: navy #1A1A2E and green #1D9E75, Balboa for headlines and slide titles, Shadows Into Light for captions and subtitles (per visual/styles/editorial-surrealism.md). The Concept Engine's creativity lives inside whichever brand system is active.
- **Lead magnet pairing:** when the carousel gates a deliverable, the kyndle-lead-magnet skill produces the matching checklist; the keyword, the deliverable name, and the cover promise must agree word-for-word.

## The Cover Design Checklist (the cover decides ~80% of reach)

1. SAY ONE THING: one cover, one message. If it says two things, it says nothing.
2. MAKE IT BIG: readable on a small phone at arm's length, or it's invisible.
3. CONTRAST IS KING: dark background + bright type never fails; grey-on-grey always does. (The luminance rule in the style file implements this both directions.)
4. CLAIM, NOT TOPIC: "typography tips" describes; "your fonts are why it looks cheap" provokes.
5. LEAVE A GAP: tease the answer, never give it away. No itch = no swipe.

Then the 3-Second Test (written/carousel-hooks.md): 5/5 or don't post. Reposting with a better cover is always allowed.

## Grading checks specific to carousels

Before design, confirm: cover passes the checklist and 3-Second Test; cover text and image are complementary, not redundant; slide 2 has its own hook and is the densest slide; slides 3-6 each end mid-thought; the CTA sits second-to-last with a concrete keyword and deliverable; the last slide works as a standalone savable receipt. Two or more failures: back to assembly.
