# Format: Graphic

Single-image posts where the image IS the entire post: statement cards, stat graphics, mini-frameworks, and promos. Platforms: Instagram feed primary; Facebook, X, and LinkedIn image-only posts secondary. (The image that accompanies a TEXT post is a different job: see the Companion Image section in formats/written-post.md, which selects from the types defined here.) Written and visual layers compressed into ONE frame with no swipe behind it, which changes the job: a cover TEASES (no itch, no swipe), but a standalone graphic usually PAYS. It's the receipt slide living alone, and its core metrics are saves and shares.

## The two hook slots

1. **The in-image text** (written/patterns.md, same goal): the claim or the payload, keyword-scaled, luminance rule applied.
2. **The caption** (separate hook, same goal): context, story, or the ramp; hook inside the platform's fold per formats/written-post.md numbers.

## The tease/pay decision (make it first)

- **PAY (default):** the graphic completes a thought worth keeping: a stat with its meaning, a one-frame framework, a checklist, a quote that stands alone. Built for the save; the caption carries the ramp.
- **TEASE:** the graphic is the tension and the caption resolves it (embodied-metaphor image + a caption that explains; a provocative claim card + the story below). Built for comments; use when the goal is virality or leads.
A graphic that half-teases and half-pays does neither. Decide, then build.

## Graphic types (from the verified systems)

1. **Statement card:** one claim, Balboa keyword-scaled, climate per the luminance rule. The silent-hook text card in static form (the 649x parallel fact stack IS this type).
2. **Stat graphic:** one number huge, two colors max, source named small at the bottom (the research protocol's receipts made visible).
3. **One-frame framework:** the receipt-slide layout from slide-system.md: titled checklist or 3-part diagram, savable.
4. **Embodied metaphor:** the Concept Engine at full power, one literalized scene, minimal text. The cover system minus the metadata dressing.
5. **Promo/announcement:** offer or launch; conversion/funnel.md governs; the only type where the ramp lives in the image itself.

## Assembly sequence

1. Goal, topic, research pass (GUIDE Step 1.5).
2. Tease or pay decision.
3. In-image text from the goal's written section; power word named; 10 words or fewer on statement cards.
4. Visual via the Concept Engine (types 4 and 5) or the slide-system layouts (types 1-3), Kyndle climates and type.
5. Caption written separately; ramp check; links in first comment on LinkedIn.
6. Grade: the 3-Second Test applies to graphics exactly as to covers. 5/5 or don't post.

## Graphic-only mechanics

- One focal element, always; a graphic has one frame to win and clutter forfeits it.
- Sizes: 4:5 feed default, 1:1 acceptable, design the grid crop.
- Brand-exact type = hybrid path (generated scenes, template typography), same as covers.
- Statement and stat cards are the fastest content in the whole system: no scenes to generate, pure template. They're the volume play between carousels.
- Quote cards quoting OTHER people need the quote to be real and attributed; made-up quotes are the visual version of invented stats. Same automatic fail.
- Series discipline: recurring graphic types (a weekly stat card, a Monday statement) keep identical layouts; the repetition is the brand, per the series consistency finding (177x/217x).

## Grading checks

Tease/pay decided; one focal; 3-Second Test 5/5; power word scaled; source named on any stat; ramp in caption (or image, promo type only); climate and type match the locked system. Two failures = back to assembly.
