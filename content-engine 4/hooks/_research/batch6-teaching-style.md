## dantebrunelli_2 (4 videos) — signature: radical transparency as the hook

**Bryan Johnson story (229.7K, 44.1x, 14% engagement)**
- VERBAL: "I hope that this story can change your life." Soft promise, then deictic grounding: "I was walking right over there on that ridge" (points at real location).
- STRUCTURE: Hook > catalyst (billionaire FaceTime) > the grind (2 months of emails/Looms/LinkedIn) > epiphany (luck = preparation meets opportunity) > hard truth ("You're going to die") > payoff > addressing critics ("I had to eat shit for two years") > permission CTA ("This is me giving you permission to take the shot"). 201s car yap.
- LESSON: The permission-giving close is a connection device inside educational content. Deictic location pointing makes a story physically real.

**Meta/Andromeda deep dive (114.8K, 23.0x)**
- VERBAL: "If you were to consume all the information I have on this piece of paper, you will know more than 99% of advertisers on Meta."
- VISUAL: Handwritten notes on physical paper as the authority prop; taps the paper while teaching.
- STRUCTURE: 14 minutes. Pipeline (Retrieval/Ranking/Auction) > the #1 mistake (creative volume) > creative diversity via Schwartz awareness stages > CTA. Client receipt embedded ($200-300K/month account).
- LESSON: "You can look all this up, it's on Meta's website" = anti-gatekeeping transparency that paradoxically builds more trust than secrecy. Ties ad strategy directly to awareness stages (Kyndle Copy OS bridge).

**5 things competitors aren't doing (35.9K, 6.7x)**
- VERBAL: List hook, then the remarkable move: "The retention on this video is probably not going to be that high because you don't get a dopamine hit and the hook wasn't super engaging. But I promise you..."
- LESSON: ANTI-HOOK HONESTY. Naming the video's own weakness as proof of substance. High-trust audiences reward this; it's a Dante signature and a differentiation play against hype marketers. Use sparingly, only with receipts behind it.

**Awareness stages breakdown (4.8K, 0.9x — delivery model only)**
- VERBAL: Scenario hypothetical: "Imagine you walk into a car dealership and the salesperson says, hey, I think I know the car for you, let's go buy it."
- STRUCTURE: Analogy > core problem (selling to cold traffic like it's warm) > Schwartz framework credited by name > comment-section CTA for the list.
- GRADING NOTE: Sandcastles flags weak bridge between text hook ("No wonder your marketing isn't working") and the spoken analogy open. Rubric would catch this: layers misaligned. Low outlier consistent with the diagnosis. Teaching content: analogy-first explanation of Schwartz, directly reusable for Kyndle.

## iamjadenly (5 videos) — signature: named frameworks + comment-keyword machine + "thank me later"

**4 YouTube videos (288K, 14.5x, 4,924 comments)**
- VERBAL: "Here are 4 YouTube videos that you must watch to instantly make better content. And here's the catch, none of these have anything to do with social media." The catch-twist re-hook lands at second 4.
- LESSON: Curating OTHER people's resources builds authority (taste as expertise). Comment "TEACH" ramp.

**Claude carousel tutorial (86.7K, 4.4x, 6,478 comments = 7.5% comment rate)**
- VERBAL: "Screw it, since everybody is marrying Claude all of a sudden, let me start a series that actually helps you use it to grow your personal brand from this to this."
- STRUCTURE: Trend-jack > series framing (part 1) > prerequisites > UI steps > master prompt template > MID-VIDEO lead magnet ("comment playbook") > reveal > series CTA.
- LESSON: Trend-jack + utility pivot ("everyone's joking about X, here's how to actually use it"). The highest comment ratio in the entire 52-video dataset. Directly relevant: it's a Claude-for-carousels tutorial, Kyndle's own workflow taught publicly.

**Stop making (2023) value content (85.8K, 4.3x)**
- VERBAL: "By far the worst thing that you can do right now is make value content. Let me explain."
- STRUCTURE: Contrarian hook > old-vs-new visual contrast > historical context (the Hormozi give-it-away era) > redefinition framework (awareness, simplification, application) > self-referential proof ("like this video").
- LESSON: Date-stamping advice "(2023)" frames the audience's current habit as outdated without insulting them. "Let me explain" is the two-word bridge that buys a contrarian claim its runway.

**Crabby Patty Formula (42.2K, 2.1x, 1,578 comments)**
- VERBAL: "Want to make ADDICTING content? 40% topic, 5% editing, 25% format/style, 30% how you communicate."
- VISUAL: Live-updating percentage table synced to speech.
- LESSON: Percentage-weighted formulas feel scientific; the 5%-for-editing surprise IS the shareable claim. 20-second video, full ramp.

**Ethical brainwash / MAES (37.8K, 1.9x)**
- VERBAL: "If you want your videos to become absolutely addicting to watch and people literally can't scroll away..."  Text hook: "Ethically BRAINWASH."
- STRUCTURE: Promise > self-experiment proof (same idea, two versions, before/after) > acronym reveal (MAES: metaphor, analogy, example, story) > psychology > retention graph proof.
- LESSON: Taboo word + ethical qualifier = permission to click. The self-experiment (A/B on his own content) is a tier-5 receipt made visible.
- SIGNATURE: every jadenly video ends "come back and thank me later," a parasocial callback that presumes the advice works. Catchphrases are retention infrastructure across videos, not within one.

## devinjatho (2 videos) — signature: negative-constraint hooks + tiered resource libraries

**Fix These 5 Settings (823K, 1.5x)**
- VERBAL: "Never post on Instagram again without turning on these five settings." Trap/negative constraint form.
- VISUAL: Greenscreen UI walkthroughs; the interface IS the b-roll.
- STRUCTURE: Trap hook > fixes 1-2 > SOCIAL PROOF BRIDGE mid-list ("these next three settings got me millions of views for free") > fixes 3-5 ("best for last") > save CTA. Caption runs comment "Nexus" agency ramp.
- LESSON: The mid-list proof bridge re-hooks exactly at the halfway droop. "Best for last" is a spoken retention promise.

**Steal Viral Background Songs (764K, 1.2x, 15,911 comments)**
- VERBAL: "These background songs go viral time and time again. Use them in your next video."
- VISUAL: Scrolling list; songs tiered by view-count level (10K > 100K > 500K > 1M > 5M).
- LESSON: Tiering the examples by results turns a list into a ladder. 150+ song library via comment "Songs": resource-library lead magnets pull the most comments per view of any format type in the dataset.
