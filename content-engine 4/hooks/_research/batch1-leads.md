## devinjatho — "You will never go viral if you don't learn how to speak on camera" (3.7M, 6.0x)
- VERBAL: Contrarian stakes warning; the SAME sentence repeated 5 times with escalating delivery (flat > pacing > tonality > gestures > emotion). The delivery IS the content.
- VISUAL: Studio talking head + live view-count progress badge (100 views > 1M) that levels up with each delivery upgrade. Text hook mirrors spoken.
- STRUCTURE: 5-stage levels format, ~4s per level. Ramp: comment "REC" for the recording process.
- LESSON: A hook can be a demonstration loop; performance layering gamifies retention. Lead magnet tied directly to the skill being demonstrated.

## hormozi x Diary of a CEO — podcast clip (1.7M, 4.9x)
- VERBAL: "I made $106 million and then my mum died." Achievement + tragedy collision in one sentence. Personal experience category, maximal stakes contrast.
- VISUAL: Serious podcast talking-head clips; tone matches hook severity exactly.
- STRUCTURE: Tragedy origin > coping method > business lesson 1 (emotion isn't a reason to change strategy) > lesson 2 (focus/patience) > AI warning. 135 seconds, long-form clip.
- RAMP: Comment "Alex" DM funnel + massive listicle caption. Caption opens with its own separate hook ("What if the biggest opportunity in AI isn't...").
- LESSON: The caption and the video can run two DIFFERENT hooks for the same piece. High-pain emotional hooks can front business content without cheapening it when receipts are real.

## edward.builds — reviews domain GEO hack (1.9M, 91.3x)
- VERBAL: "If you have any type of business, buy a domain name: your business name plus the word reviews." Tutorial command hook; the instruction IS the hook, no preamble.
- VISUAL: Walking rooftop vlog, skyline = status signal. Location matched to advice level.
- STRUCTURE: Command > implementation detail > the strategic why (ChatGPT due-diligence behavior) > the critical catch (link from your own site). 38 seconds.
- LESSON: "Command-first" tutorial hooks work when the action is cheap and the stakes are high. The "one catch" section near the end is a retention anchor.

## viralclubhouse_ — "Delete that video and post it again" (7M, 150.2x)
- VERBAL: Contrarian command REPEATED twice verbatim. Rhythmic repetition reads as urgent instruction.
- VISUAL: Low-fi couch intimacy, direct eye contact, text overlay mirrors the command.
- STRUCTURE: Command x2 > logic (no views = nobody saw it) > proof (500 to 500,000) > simplification ("you're overcomplicating this, just do it"). 28 seconds.
- LESSON: Repetition of the opening line is a legit verbal device (2 confirmed uses now with devinjatho's loop). Casual set can outperform polish when the advice feels like insider talk.

## haydengarlock — 3 wedding business ideas (3.1M, 177.1x)
- VERBAL: "Here are three business ideas for weddings that will make you a millionaire and how much each of them can make you per year." List promise + money metric baked into the hook itself.
- VISUAL: Car talking-head + earnings overlays and product images appearing per item.
- STRUCTURE: Hook > idea 1 with price floor + annual number > idea 2 > idea 3 with personal proof (wife did it) > follow CTA. 32 seconds.
- LESSON: Numbers-per-item overlays are the visual engine of money listicles. Personal proof attached to ONE list item (not all) reads authentic.
