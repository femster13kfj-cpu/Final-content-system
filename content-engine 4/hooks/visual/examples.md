# Visual Hook Examples

Verified visual hooks with numbers. Entry format: what the eye sees in frame one, numbers + link, families used, why it worked, ADAPT IT. Same rule as every examples file: no numbers, no entry.

**Kids mid-leap onto dad in a pool; text card: "all he wanted was my number and now this is his life"**
150.1M views, 4,220x. https://app.sandcastles.ai/video/7bd34744-f780-4485-84e9-9f7e0f9270e3
Families: action + text. The chaotic now under the simple-past premise; the contrast is the entire hook, zero speech.
ADAPT IT: film your real chaos, caption it with the innocent decision that started it.

ADAPT IT: pick one culturally loaded word in your niche and act its rise and fall in 10 seconds.
